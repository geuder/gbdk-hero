# Credits

## Art

- **Diver sprites:** "8-Directional Game Boy Character Template" by **GibbonGL**,
  <https://gibbongl.itch.io/8-directional-gameboy-character-template>.
  It is released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Credit isn't required, but it is given
  gladly. The sheet is in `assets/hero.png`, and the frames are cut out by
  `tools/make_gfx.py`. The lying-down frame used for the death animation is one of
  these frames, rotated.
- **Everything else** (seabed, rocks, pumps, fish, bubbles, gems and sparkle, hook, HUD,
  font) is drawn in code in `tools/make_gfx.py`, specifically for this project.

The in-game title screen also shows the credit: `HERO ART: GIBBONGL CC0`.
