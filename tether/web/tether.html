Swim with the D-pad. The diver's air hose runs straight from the water pump and catches on rocks it swings across; swim back around a rock and it comes free. Touch another pump to switch to it: the hose reels in from the old pump to the diver, then attaches to the new pump. Reeling uses air. Fish bite the hose: the first bites use up its HP, after that every bite makes a hole that leaks 1 air per second. At 0 air the diver drowns. Collect every gem, then swim back to the start pump to be lifted out. Five levels. Each level has its own hose length; the slider overrides it.

ARROWS move, R reset, L next level, SPACE reach circle.
